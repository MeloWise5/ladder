## Animation Prompt

> **Scene-by-scene explainer animation — 60 seconds — dark finance/tech aesthetic**
>
> **Visual style:** Sleek dark-mode UI mockups. Deep navy-blue background (#0d1b2a). Neon accent colors — teal (#00d4b4) for buy actions, coral/orange (#ff6b47) for sell actions, soft white (#f0f4f8) for text. Smooth ease-in-out transitions. Clean sans-serif typography. Subtle particle/grid overlay to suggest a live trading environment.
>
> ---
>
> **SCENE 1 — 0:00–0:08 | Hook: The Problem**
>
> Camera slowly zooms into a glowing crypto price chart spiking and crashing. Numbers blur past. A lone trader stares at a screen looking overwhelmed. Text fades in:
> *"Markets move fast. Manual trading misses the window."*
> Hard cut to black.
>
> ---
>
> **SCENE 2 — 0:08–0:18 | Introducing Ladder**
>
> The word **"LADDER"** builds itself letter-by-letter from glowing teal lines, like rungs of a ladder assembling upward. A clean browser window slides in showing the Ladder dashboard — a dark-mode trading interface with multiple colored ladder cards (each card displays a coin symbol, a profit number, and a status badge). Voiceover/caption:
> *"Ladder automates your buy-low, sell-high strategy — hands-free."*
>
> ---
>
> **SCENE 3 — 0:18–0:30 | How a Ladder Works**
>
> Animated vertical price chart on the left. On the right, a "ladder" diagram appears — horizontal steps stacked at different price levels:
>
> - Step 1 highlighted in teal: **"Buy at $95,000"** — a buy order fires with a teal pulse animation and a ✓ tick.
> - Price drops to $92,000 — Step 2 lights up: **"Buy at $92,000"** — another pulse.
> - Price rises back to $97,000 — both steps flip to coral/orange: **"Sell at $97,000"** — profit counter ticks upward (+$310.00).
>
> Caption: *"Set your price steps once. Ladder handles every trade automatically."*
>
> ---
>
> **SCENE 4 — 0:30–0:40 | Live Dashboard**
>
> Screen recording–style flythrough of the Ladder web app UI:
>
> 1. **Dashboard view** — grid of ladder cards with tickers (BTC, ETH, SOL, AAPL), each showing current price, profit/loss badge, and an active/paused toggle.
> 2. **Ladder detail page** — step table animates in row by row; filled steps glow teal, open steps pulse softly. A transaction history table scrolls with recent trades.
> 3. **Charts panel** — a smooth line chart draws itself showing daily P&L over 30 days trending upward.
>
> Caption: *"Track every trade, every profit, in real time."*
>
> ---
>
> **SCENE 5 — 0:40–0:50 | AI Suggestions + Exchange Integration**
>
> Split screen:
> - Left: A glowing OpenAI-style brain icon sends a suggestion card into the UI: *"BTC ladder — consider adding a step at $88,000 based on support levels."*
> - Right: A Coinbase logo connects via an animated data-pipe to the Ladder app; order confirmation badges (BUY FILLED / SELL FILLED) pop in sequence.
>
> Caption: *"AI-powered suggestions. Direct Coinbase integration."*
>
> ---
>
> **SCENE 6 — 0:50–0:58 | Call to Action**
>
> Zoom out to show the full app on a laptop screen sitting on a clean desk. The profit counter on the screen keeps ticking upward. The Ladder logo pulses once. Text overlays:
>
> - **"Set your ladder."**
> - **"Let it trade."**
> - **"Watch it grow."**
>
> Final frame: URL or logo lockup on the dark background with a subtle teal glow.
>
> ---
>
> **SCENE 7 — 0:58–1:00 | Logo Outro**
>
> The LADDER wordmark re-assembles from teal rungs, holds for 2 seconds, fades out.
